T001 · Top-down 与 bottom-up

Compare top-down and bottom-up approaches to network design.

比较两种设计方法。

Moodle · Week 1 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 39–40 · Network Design Methodology

参考答案与解析
中文思路

先业务目标还是先技术；需求匹配、既有约束与反馈。

A top-down approach starts with the organization’s goals and the needs of users and applications. The designer develops service requirements, traffic flows and architecture before selecting detailed technologies and equipment. This helps the network support the business.

A bottom-up approach begins with technologies, topologies or products. It can be convenient when a designer already knows particular equipment, but it may produce a network that fails to meet the real requirements.

For example, buying faster switches first will not solve every application problem. The actual problem may be delay on an external link or poor server performance. A top-down process investigates that need first. Existing equipment and technical limits still matter, so the process allows feedback. The key is to justify technology choices through requirements rather than personal preference.

易错／变式：top-down 不表示完全忽略现有设备。

T002 · PPDIOO 生命周期

Describe the six phases of PPDIOO and explain why network design is a life-cycle process.

说明六阶段及持续循环。

Moodle · Week 1 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 45–46 · PPDIOO

参考答案与解析
中文思路

Prepare→Plan→Design→Implement→Operate→Optimize；优化反馈，不是装完结束。

PPDIOO describes six phases: Prepare, Plan, Design, Implement, Operate and Optimize. Prepare establishes business goals and requirements. Plan gathers information, analyses needs and identifies an approach. Design develops the network topology and solution using requirements and knowledge of the existing network.

Implement puts the design into practice by installing and configuring the required solution. Operate manages and monitors the deployed network. Optimize uses operational evidence to improve the design or respond to problems and new requirements.

The phases form a life cycle rather than a process that ends after installation. For example, a new teaching application may expose a capacity problem during operation. Optimization may lead back to planning or design before further changes are implemented. This connects ongoing service quality with the organization’s changing goals and preserves the need for verification after deployment.

易错／变式：PPDIOO有两个O：Operate和Optimize；不要漏Prepare或把Plan/Design混同。

T003 · Eight-step design methodology

Describe the eight-step network design methodology and its documentation.

说明八步方法与文档用途。

The eight steps are: identify customer requirements, describe the existing network, design the topology and solution, plan implementation, construct a prototype, fully document the design, implement it, and verify, monitor and modify it as needed.

T004 · 分析、架构、设计的联系

Explain how network analysis, architecture and design are connected.

解释三阶段怎样相互连接。

Moodle · Week 1 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 57–64 · Information flows

中文思路

Network analysis identifies what users, applications and devices need. It studies the current network, traffic, service levels and constraints. Its results give reasons for later decisions.

T005 · 网络要素与设备

Describe the main elements of a computer network and their roles.

说明终端、中间设备和介质的作用。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 5–19 · Network elements

参考答案与解析
中文思路

host 起止；switch 局域网转发；router 跨网络；AP无线接入；介质传信号。

A computer network connects end devices through communication media and intermediate devices. End devices, also called hosts, create or receive messages. Examples include computers, servers and printers.

Intermediate devices help messages reach their destinations. A switch usually forwards Ethernet frames within a local network. A router forwards IP packets between networks. An access point allows wireless devices to join a network. Other devices, such as repeaters and modems, have different connection or signal functions.

The media carry signals and may include copper cables, optical fibre or wireless links. Protocols and addresses make communication possible across these elements. A design must consider how the elements work together. For example, a fast backbone does not remove a slow wireless access link or a server bottleneck at the end of a path.

T006 · 网络类别

Explain LAN, WAN, WLAN, CAN, MAN, SAN and VPN.

T007 · 网络组织模式

Compare centralized, client-server, distributed, peer-to-peer, cloud-based and hybrid networks.

A centralized network places major control or processing at a central computer. A client-server network gives clients and servers different roles: clients request services and servers provide them. Central services can simplify management but may create important points of failure.

T008 · 拓扑与故障

Compare mesh, tree, bus, star and ring topologies.

比较五种拓扑及成本、故障影响。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 28–34 · Network Topology

参考答案与解析
中文思路

连接结构 → 备用路径与故障范围；物理/逻辑区分。

A star connects devices to a central device. It is easy to extend, but failure of the central device may affect many users. Failure of one edge link usually affects only its attached device.

A mesh provides multiple connections and alternative paths. It can improve resilience, but it needs more links, ports and management. A tree organizes connections in a hierarchy. It supports growth, although a failure near the root can affect a large branch. A bus uses a shared backbone, so faults or contention on that shared medium can affect many devices. A ring connects devices in a loop; failure effects depend on whether protection or an alternative path exists.

Physical topology describes actual connections. Logical topology describes communication relationships. A suitable choice balances cost, scale, traffic and failure requirements.

易错／变式：不能说任何 ring 断一条链路都必然全网停机。

T009 · Protocol 的规则

What is a network protocol, and why must communicating devices follow common rules?

什么是协议，为什么必须遵循共同规则？

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 36–46 · Network Protocol

参考答案与解析
中文思路

格式、含义、顺序、时序、发送接收动作。

A network protocol defines rules for exchanging messages between communicating entities. It specifies message formats, the meaning of fields, the order of exchanges and the actions taken when a message or event occurs.

Common rules are needed for encoding, message size, encapsulation, timing and delivery. For example, devices must know how to recognize a message, when to respond and what to do if a response does not arrive. Timing rules may include flow control, response timeouts and access to a shared medium.

Protocols can be implemented in software, hardware or both. Different protocols perform different tasks, such as application communication, routing and security. They must work together along a communication path. Without agreed rules, two devices might be physically connected but still be unable to understand or correctly process each other’s messages.

易错／变式：protocol 不只是设备之间的一根线或一种语言名称。

T010 · OSI、TCP/IP 与封装

Describe the OSI and TCP/IP models and explain encapsulation.

描述分层模型与封装过程。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 47–57、85–89 · Network Protocol Models

参考答案与解析
中文思路

OSI 七层；TCP/IP 四层；发端加头、收端逐层解释移除。

The OSI model has seven layers: application, presentation, session, transport, network, data link and physical. Each layer has a particular communication role and uses services from the layer below.

The TCP/IP model in this course has four layers: application, transport, Internet and network access. Its application layer covers several upper OSI functions. Its network access layer covers data-link and physical communication.

During encapsulation, application data passes down the stack. Transport and Internet protocols add information needed for process delivery and routing. The link layer places the packet in a frame, and the physical layer sends signals. At the receiver, corresponding layers interpret and remove the relevant information. Layering separates responsibilities and helps troubleshooting. It does not mean that each model layer always corresponds to one separate physical device.

易错／变式：区分 packet、frame 和 signal；TCP/IP 本课用四层。

T011 · 应用协议、端口与 socket

Explain the roles of application protocols, ports and sockets.

解释应用层协议与进程定位。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 58–60、66–67 · Application Layer

参考答案与解析
中文思路

应用协议规定业务消息；IP到host；port到应用端点；socket 接口。

Application protocols define how applications exchange meaningful messages. HTTP supports web requests and responses, DNS translates names into network information, and SMTP supports sending email. FTP supports file transfer, while POP and IMAP support email access.

An IP address identifies a network interface for packet delivery. A transport port helps identify the application endpoint on a host. For example, HTTP commonly uses TCP port 80 and HTTPS commonly uses TCP port 443. A socket is an application’s communication endpoint.

Multiplexing collects data from different sockets for transmission. Demultiplexing delivers received data to the correct socket using transport information. This allows several applications to share the network at the same time. A web browser is an application; HTTP is a protocol that the browser and server use. They are related but are not the same thing.

T012 · TCP 与 UDP

Compare TCP and UDP and explain when each may be useful.

比较传输层两种协议。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 61–68 · Transport Layer

参考答案与解析
中文思路

TCP可靠有序、连接、流控拥塞控；UDP无这些内建保证，应用取舍。

TCP provides connection-oriented communication and a reliable, ordered byte stream. It uses sequence numbers, acknowledgements and retransmission to handle missing data. It also includes flow control and congestion control.

UDP sends datagrams without setting up a TCP-style connection. It does not itself guarantee delivery, ordering or retransmission. This makes its service simpler, but an application may need to provide its own protection against loss or congestion.

TCP is useful when complete and ordered delivery matters, such as transferring a file. UDP can suit applications that prefer timely delivery or use their own communication mechanisms. However, UDP is not automatically fast enough for every real-time application, and TCP does not guarantee low delay. The designer must consider application tolerance for loss, delay, overhead and the behaviour of the complete path.

易错／变式：不要写 UDP 保证实时、TCP保证固定延迟。

T013 · Forwarding、routing 与各层地址

Distinguish forwarding from routing and explain link-layer and network-layer delivery.

区分转发与路由，以及链路层和网络层。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 69–89 · Network Layer

参考答案与解析
中文思路

routing算路径/学习可达；forwarding查表送下一跳；MAC逐链路、IP跨网络。

Routing learns network reachability and selects paths. Routing information is used to build forwarding information. Forwarding is the local action of moving an arriving packet to the correct outgoing interface or next hop.

At the network layer, IP addresses identify source and destination interfaces across networks. At the data-link layer, a frame carries data across a particular local link. Ethernet uses MAC addresses for this local delivery. The physical layer carries the bits as signals.

When a router forwards a packet onto another link, it usually creates a new link-layer frame for that next link. The final IP destination normally remains the same unless a mechanism such as NAT changes it. This distinction helps explain why routers need both network-layer forwarding information and suitable link-layer information for their next-hop communication.

易错／变式：不要把 forwarding 的逐包动作与 routing 的路径计算互换。

T014 · Router 四部件

Describe the four main components of a router and their roles.

解释路由器内部四个主要组成。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 71–76 · Network Layer - Router

参考答案与解析
中文思路

input ports接收；fabric内部搬运；output ports缓冲/发出；processor控制。

A router has input ports, output ports, a switching fabric and a routing processor. Input ports receive traffic and perform the relevant physical- and link-layer functions. They also participate in forwarding operations.

The switching fabric connects input ports to the appropriate output ports inside the router. Output ports store packets when needed and transmit them onto outgoing links using the required link and physical functions. Queuing can occur when arriving traffic exceeds the available transmission service.

The routing processor performs control-plane work. In a traditional router, it runs routing protocols, maintains routing information and helps compute forwarding tables. In an SDN design, the device can receive forwarding information through communication with a controller. These roles explain the distinction between deciding forwarding behaviour and carrying out local packet forwarding. Processing, fabric and interface limits can each affect performance.

Compare centralized/decentralized, static/dynamic and load-sensitive/load-insensitive routing algorithms.

T016 · Link、physical 与 service model

Describe link-layer and physical-layer services and distinguish them from an end-to-end network service model.

区分逐链路、物理传输与端到端服务承诺。

Moodle · Week 2 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 86–89 · Network service model

参考答案与解析
中文思路

frame相邻节点；bits介质信号；可靠性视协议；endtoend不由一个hop保证。

The data-link layer transfers frames between adjacent nodes on a link. Its functions can include framing, access to the medium, error detection and correction, and, for some link protocols, reliable local delivery. A device uses a network interface to connect to a link.

The physical layer transmits bits through a medium, such as copper, optical fibre or wireless communication. Its operation depends on that medium and the associated signalling rules.

An end-to-end network service model describes the delivery characteristics offered between sender and receiver. Possible characteristics include delivery, bounded delay, ordering, minimum bandwidth or security. Best effort does not promise all these properties. A reliable local link is therefore different from guaranteed end-to-end delivery. Every relevant part of the path, and possibly the endpoints, must support the application’s actual service needs.

易错／变式：不是所有link protocol都提供可靠交付；某一跳可靠不代表端到端保证。

T113 · SDN的优点

What are advantages of SDN?

SDN有哪些优点？

Moodle · Week 12 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 8–22 · SDN Features

参考答案与解析
中文思路

SDN分离控制平面与数据平面。转发设备按规则处理流量，软件控制决定怎样管理规则，因此更容易改变网络行为，无需逐一单独配置每台设备。

可编程性让应用通过接口请求网络功能，能够支持更快配置变化、自动化以及多设备间更一致的政策。控制器较完整的网络视角也有助于协调流量处理与资源使用，而不只依靠孤立设备的决定。

分离允许控制软件与转发设备较独立地演进，使引入服务和适应需求变化更灵活。

收益取决于适当设计和实现。控制器可用性、安全、扩展性和接口兼容仍重要。逻辑集中不要求只有一台物理控制器，SDN也不自动保证更高速度或完全安全；应依据需求评价其价值。

SDN separates the control plane from the data plane. Forwarding devices handle traffic according to rules, while software-based control decides how those rules are managed. This makes it easier to change network behaviour without configuring every device separately.

Programmability allows applications to request network functions through interfaces. It can support faster configuration changes, automation and more consistent policies across many devices. A controller's broader network view can also help coordinate traffic handling and resource use instead of relying only on isolated device decisions.

The separation allows control software and forwarding devices to evolve more independently. It can make introducing new services and adapting to changing requirements more flexible.

These benefits depend on suitable design and implementation. Controller availability, security, scalability and interface compatibility still matter. Logically centralized control does not require a single physical controller, and SDN does not automatically guarantee higher speed or complete security. Its value should be judged against the network's requirements.

易错／变式：只列“快、便宜、安全”；把controller当作所有数据包必经转发设备；把逻辑集中当物理单点。

T114 · SDN 三平面

Describe the three planes of SDN and how they interact.

描述SDN三个平面的分工。

Moodle · Week 12 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 8–20 · SDN Architecture

参考答案与解析
中文思路

application意图/服务；controller全局策略；data匹配转发/状态回报。

SDN separates the application, control and data planes. The application plane contains network services and applications that express requirements and use a view of the network.

The control plane is managed by one or more controllers. It maintains information about the infrastructure, makes or implements policy decisions and programs forwarding behaviour. The data plane contains physical or virtual forwarding devices. They process traffic according to rules and report relevant network state.

The separation allows control logic and forwarding to evolve more independently. For example, an application can request a traffic policy, the controller translates it into device rules, and switches apply those rules to packets. This supports programmability and coordinated management. A logically centralized view does not require one unprotected physical controller. Controller architecture, communication and failure handling remain important design decisions.

易错／变式：application plane不是交换机端口；controller不等于逐个代替交换机传所有数据。

T115 · SDN interfaces 与 OpenFlow

Explain northbound, southbound and east–west interfaces in SDN.

解释三类接口及OpenFlow的角色。

Moodle · Week 12 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 12–20 · Interfaces of an SDN controller

参考答案与解析
中文思路

north应用↔control；south控制↔设备；eastwest控制器；OpenFlow一种south协议。

A northbound interface connects the SDN control plane with applications or higher-level services. It lets applications obtain network information and express requirements. A southbound interface connects controllers with forwarding infrastructure so that control decisions can be applied to devices.

An east–west interface supports communication between controllers in a distributed control architecture. OpenFlow is one example of a southbound protocol; it is not the only possible SDN interface or the complete definition of SDN.

In an OpenFlow-style system, a switch matches packets against flow-table entries and performs the associated actions, such as forwarding or dropping. Controller involvement depends on installed rules and configuration. The interfaces must support the needed functions, security and scale. Different northbound APIs can also create portability and integration challenges between applications and control platforms.

T120 · NFV 与三要素

Define NFV and describe NFVI, VNFs and MANO.

定义NFV及三项架构元素。

Moodle · Week 12 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 35–38 · NFV Architecture

中文思路

软件网络功能脱离专有盒子；infrastructure资源；VNF功能；MANO管理编排。

Network Function Virtualization, or NFV, implements network functions as software on shared, general-purpose infrastructure. For example, a firewall or load-balancing function can run as a software instance instead of requiring a separate dedicated appliance for every deployment.

The course identifies three main elements. NFV infrastructure, or NFVI, provides the computing, storage, networking and virtualization resources. T121 · SDN 与 NFV 的区别和组合

Compare SDN and NFV and explain how they can work together.

比较SDN/NFV并说明组合方式。

Moodle · Week 12 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 36–39 · Relation of SDN and NFV

参考答案与解析
中文思路

SDN分离control/data；NFV分离function/专用设备；一个管连接，一个软件化功能。

SDN separates network control from data forwarding and makes forwarding behaviour programmable. NFV separates network functions from dedicated physical appliances and runs those functions as software on shared infrastructure.

They address different forms of flexibility. SDN concerns how traffic is controlled and connected. NFV concerns how services such as firewalls or load balancers are implemented and deployed. One does not automatically require the other.

The combined design must still consider capacity, availability, security and coordination. T122 · AI、ML、DL 与学习类型

Explain AI, ML and DL and compare supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.

解释包含关系及三类学习。

Moodle · Week 12 - Lecture Slides · PDF物理页 40–44 · ML model categories

参考答案与解析
中文思路

AI⊃ML⊃DL；有标签、无标签找结构、动作奖励；不是都需人工标签。

Artificial intelligence is the broader field of systems performing tasks associated with intelligent behaviour. Machine learning is a part of AI that learns patterns from data or experience. Deep learning is a part of ML that uses multi-layer neural-network approaches.

T124 · 安全、隐私与 Threat analysis

Discuss the importance of security and privacy in strategic network design. Why is threat analysis important in developing a security and privacy plan? What should threat analysis consider? Give three examples of threats.

讨论重要性、威胁分析的作用、考虑因素，并给三个威胁例子。

Moodle · Week 13 - Lecture Slides (Summary) · PDF物理页 17 · Example – Essay

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 5–14 · Three Classic Security Considerations

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 14–21 · Threat analysis

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 22–28 · Policies and Procedures

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 29–35 · Physical Security

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 32–37 · Protocol & Application Security

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 38–50 · Network Perimeter Security

Moodle · Week 6 - Lecture Slides (b) · PDF物理页 42–50 · External Relationship

参考答案与解析

Introduction

战略网络设计应长期支持组织目标。安全与隐私很重要，因为用户依赖网络获取信息、通信和提供服务。网络即使容量足够，如果信息被盗、被修改或不可用，也可能无法实现目标。因此应在需求分析与架构阶段考虑安全，而不是等选完设备再补加。有效计划连接业务需要、重要资产、可能威胁、适当控制和清楚责任。威胁分析为这些选择提供依据，并帮助解释为什么网络特定部分需要保护。

Strategic network design should support an organization's goals over time. Security and privacy are important because users depend on the network to access information, communicate and provide services. A network may have enough capacity but still fail its purpose if information is stolen, changed or unavailable. Security should therefore be considered during requirements analysis and architecture development, rather than added only after equipment has been selected. A useful plan connects business needs, important assets, possible threats, suitable controls and clear responsibilities. Threat analysis provides evidence for these choices and helps explain why protection is needed in particular parts of the network.

Importance of security and privacy

CIA目标是一个有用起点。保密性防止未授权访问和披露；完整性防止未授权修改；可用性表示需要时能访问资源和服务。在本课程中，隐私被视为安全的一部分，尤其关注防止未授权访问和披露。大学的学生记录需要保密性和完整性，在线教学还需要可用性。失效可能中断教学、损害信任并带来恢复费用。用户、应用和设备可能有不同需要，因此保护应依据需求与风险，而非在所有地方采用完全相同安排。

The CIA goals provide a useful starting point. Confidentiality protects information from unauthorized access and disclosure. Integrity protects information from unauthorized changes. Availability means that resources and services are accessible when required. In this subject, privacy is treated as part of security, especially protection against unauthorized access and disclosure. For a university, student records need confidentiality and integrity, while online teaching also needs availability. Failure can interrupt teaching, damage trust and create recovery costs. Different users, applications and devices may have different needs, so protection should be based on requirements and risk rather than applied identically everywhere.

Why threat analysis supports the plan

威胁分析识别要保护什么，以及什么可能损害它。没有分析，设计者可能选择熟悉的产品，却遗漏重要风险。分析把威胁连接到资产和服务，考虑每个事件的发生可能性，并估计可能损害。这些结果支持优先级判断。发生可能性高且后果严重的威胁需要适当回应；较低优先级威胁可能采用不同处理程度。目的是在有限资源下，把风险降至可接受水平，同时支持组织主要目标。

Threat analysis identifies what must be protected and what could harm it. Without this analysis, a designer may choose familiar products while leaving important risks untreated. The analysis connects threats to assets and services, considers the likelihood of each event and estimates the possible damage. These findings support priorities. A threat with both high likelihood and serious consequences needs a suitable response. A lower-priority threat may receive a different level of treatment. The purpose is to reduce risk to an acceptable level while using limited resources in a way that supports the organization's main goals.

From analysis to action

分析结果也指导政策、机制和责任。例如，未授权访问风险可以引出身份验证和访问规则，物理损害风险则可能需要受保护的机房与恢复安排。这是针对不同问题的不同回应。计划应说明由谁落实控制、怎样检查以及事件后怎么办。分析还使取舍变得清楚：某些控制增加处理、延迟、成本或管理工作。设计者应结合性能与管理要求评估这些影响，而不是假定增加更多控制就总能得到最好的整体设计。

The results also guide policies, mechanisms and responsibilities. For example, a risk of unauthorized access may lead to identity checks and access rules, while a risk of physical damage may require protected rooms and recovery arrangements. These are different responses to different problems. The plan should explain who applies each control, how it is checked and what happens after an incident. Analysis also makes trade-offs visible. Some controls add processing, delay, cost or management work. Designers should evaluate these effects with the performance and management requirements instead of assuming that adding more controls always gives the best overall design.

What to consider: assets and threats

第一步是列出资产并理解其重要性，包括用户硬件、服务器、网络设备、软件、服务和数据，还应考虑位置、用户和依赖。支持关键活动的服务可能比次要服务需要更强的可用性保护。接着识别相关威胁，例如未授权访问、披露、拒绝服务、盗窃、数据破坏、恶意软件和物理损害。每项威胁应连接具体资产。只列攻击名称，不能说明什么处于风险中，以及事件如何影响组织。

The first task is to list assets and understand their importance. Assets include user hardware, servers, network devices, software, services and data. Their locations, users and dependencies should be considered. A service that supports an essential activity may deserve stronger availability protection than a less important service. The next task is to identify relevant threats, such as unauthorized access, disclosure, denial of service, theft, data corruption, malicious software and physical damage. Each threat should be connected to specific assets. A list of attack names alone does not show what is at risk or how an incident would affect the organization.

What to consider: likelihood, impact and review

对每项相关威胁，分析应考虑发生可能性及发生后的费用或损害。影响取决于业务目标，可以包括工作中断、数据丢失、恢复工作量和用户损害。现有弱点和控制措施有助于解释评估及额外行动需要。如果没有准确概率，应说明假设，而不是把它们包装成精确事实。分析含有判断，因此应让不同群体参与。组织应定期复查，尤其在用户、服务、设备或连接改变时；旧评估可能不再反映当前需要。

For each relevant threat, the analysis should consider likelihood and the cost or damage if it occurs. Impact depends on business goals and may include interrupted work, lost data, recovery effort and harm to users. Existing weaknesses and controls can help explain the assessment and the additional action required. Where exact probabilities are unavailable, assumptions should be stated clearly rather than presented as precise facts. Different groups should contribute because threat analysis includes judgement. The organization should review the assessment periodically, especially when users, services, equipment or network connections change. An old assessment may no longer describe the current needs.

Example 1: unauthorized access

第一个例子是未授权访问学生记录。资产是记录服务及其数据。有人利用保护不当的账户，可能读取隐私信息或修改成绩；读取影响保密性，改分影响完整性。分析应检查访问如何发生、能接触哪些记录以及后果多严重。计划可结合身份认证、授权、受保护通信和访问监测。认证检查身份，授权限制允许行为。加密可保护传输中数据，但本身不能阻止已有合法访问权限者的滥用。

The first example is unauthorized access to student records. The asset is the records service and its data. Someone using an improperly protected account could read private information or change grades. Reading affects confidentiality, while changing grades affects integrity. The analysis should examine how access could occur, which records could be reached and how serious the consequences would be. The plan can combine authentication with authorization, protected communication and access monitoring. Authentication checks identity; authorization limits permitted actions. Encryption can protect data in transit, but it does not by itself stop misuse by someone who already has valid access.

Example 2: denial of service

第二个例子是针对在线教学系统的拒绝服务。资产是教学服务及其所需网络资源。大量不需要的流量可能占用容量或处理资源，使合法用户无法上课，主要威胁可用性。分析应考虑重要教学时段、共享链路和会话中断的后果。回应可包括流量过滤、监测、明确的事件响应与恢复流程。有效程度取决于事件与设计；不能把一台防火墙写成防住所有拒绝服务攻击的保证。

The second example is denial of service against an online teaching system. The asset is the teaching service and the network resources it needs. A large amount of unwanted traffic could consume capacity or processing resources and prevent legitimate users from joining classes. This mainly threatens availability. The analysis should consider important teaching periods, shared links and the consequences of interrupted sessions. Appropriate responses may include traffic filtering, monitoring and a defined incident response and recovery process. Their effectiveness depends on the event and the design. A single firewall should not be presented as a guarantee against every denial-of-service attack.

Example 3: malicious software

第三个例子是破坏文件或使服务器停用的恶意软件。资产包括存储成果、应用和受影响设备上运行的服务。破坏文件影响完整性，中断服务影响可用性。分析应识别软件如何进入、可能传播多广，以及哪些服务依赖该服务器。计划可包括维护与补丁程序、适当访问限制、监测、备份与恢复安排，并明确责任人。备份支持恢复，但不能防止所有感染，也不自动保证全部服务立即恢复。

The third example is malicious software that corrupts files or disables a server. The assets include stored work, applications and the services running on the affected device. Corruption can damage integrity, and interruption can damage availability. The analysis should identify how the software might enter, how widely it could spread and which services depend on that server. The plan can include maintenance and patching procedures, appropriate access restrictions, monitoring and backups with recovery arrangements. These actions should have clear owners. Strategic planning 与 Crouch

A strategic network plan connects long-term organizational goals with actions and resources. It asks why the organization exists, how it operates, where it is now, where it wants to be, how it will get there and how it will know that it has arrived.

These questions connect mission and values with the current situation, future direction and measures of success. Gap analysis identifies the difference between the present and desired states. Strategy sets the broad direction and deployment of resources. Tactics describe more specific actions used to carry out that direction.

For example, a university may aim to support teaching across several campuses. Its plan must connect that aim to network reach, service quality, budgets, staff and milestones. A plan is useful only if people can implement it, monitor progress and revise it when conditions change.

需求分析五步

The process begins by gathering and listing requirements from users, management, the current network and initial project conditions. The designer then develops service metrics so that needs can be measured and checked.

Next, user and application behaviour is characterized. This includes user numbers, session frequency, duration, data sizes and traffic patterns. Requirements are then developed into more precise capacity, delay and RMA needs. Finally, the requirements are mapped to locations to show where services and performance are required.

The main output is a maintained requirements specification, supported by measurements and maps. The activities may be revisited when new information appears. Flow 的定义与建立

A traffic flow is a set of network traffic with common attributes, such as source, destination, information type and direction. It is an end-to-end entity that can be associated with an application, device or user.

To develop flows, identify application requirements and locations, locate data sources and sinks, apply suitable flow models and combine performance requirements into flow specifications. A source produces data, while a sink receives it. The same device may perform both roles.

An individual flow represents a single application session. A composite flow combines flows sharing a link, path or network. A critical flow has particular importance or strict requirements. A flow map connects requirements to places in the network. This helps reveal shared paths, bottlenecks and where service mechanisms must be applied.

Internal relationships exist between mechanisms within one component architecture. They include trade-offs, dependencies and constraints. For example, within performance architecture, a policy may depend on QoS mechanisms to enforce a service level agreement.

External relationships exist between different component architectures. For example, security inspection can affect performance, management traffic depends on addressing and routing, and management access can create security exposure.

The classification depends on the functions being compared, not simply whether the devices are in the same building. A reference architecture combines the component architectures and their relationships. It must balance them according to requirements and flow priorities. Optimizing each component alone may give a poor overall result. For instance, extensive inspection may improve one security goal while making a critical application miss its delay requirement.

Develop a consistent rating method. Criteria may include cost, technology, performance, risk and scalability. Weights should reflect agreed priorities, and scores should be supported by comparable evidence. Ratings and ranking should be made independently of the vendors being assessed.

The preferred option must meet essential requirements and provide a suitable overall balance. Purchase price alone is not enough: support, reputation, backup resources, operating costs and compatibility also matter. Keep the process transparent and document why candidates are accepted or rejected. If the evidence shows that no candidate meets a primary requirement, review the options or requirements rather than hiding that failure inside a favourable average score.

Physical blueprints describe actual devices, cables, rooms and security locations. Component plans isolate a function, such as security or network management, for clearer explanation.

Success metrics check whether the design achieves its goals. Capacity, delay and RMA are important, but user and device requirements may add other measures. For example, a teaching-service requirement should connect to a traffic policy, suitable devices and a defined performance test. Introduction

先说明本题的目标与主线。

A performance architecture should give the right service to the right traffic. Interactive lessons, critical transactions and backups share resources but do not have identical needs. The aim is therefore to define measurable requirements, identify where flows compete and select mechanisms that are necessary and sufficient. Extra features are useful only if they solve an agreed problem and can be operated over time. Capacity planning and service differentiation should be considered together, because neither a fast link nor a priority marking alone proves that an application receives the required end-to-end service.

Capacity、throughput、delay 与 jitter

capacity潜力、throughput实得、delay单次传送、jitter变化、TCT整任务。

Capacity is the data-carrying capability of a network resource. Throughput is the rate of useful transfer actually achieved under particular conditions. A high-capacity link does not mean an application always receives that rate.

Delay is the time taken for data or a response to travel through the system. One-way delay and round-trip time describe different paths. Jitter is variation in delay. Task completion time is the time needed to finish an entire application task, such as transferring a file.

Data size divided by the allowed transfer time gives a basic average rate requirement after units are converted. Actual planning must also consider overhead, peaks, competing flows and end-system limits. Real-time applications may be sensitive to delay and jitter even when average throughput is adequate. Application duration should not be confused with packet delay.

Flowspec 与聚合规则

BE合容量；predictable合容量取最严格delay/RMA；guaranteed逐流保留。

A flow specification lists flows and their performance requirements. A one-part flowspec contains best-effort traffic and combines its capacity requirements. A two-part flowspec includes predictable traffic and may also include best effort.

For predictable traffic, capacity requirements are combined, while the most demanding relevant delay and RMA requirements must be preserved. In the course’s aggregation method, this means the minimum permitted delay and maximum required reliability or availability value. It does not mean adding availability percentages.

A multipart flowspec also includes guaranteed flows. Each guaranteed flow keeps its own requirements rather than losing them inside a single average. This is important because an acceptable aggregate may hide failure to meet one flow’s commitment. Aggregation must also respect shared paths, direction and timing. Opposite-direction or non-overlapping traffic should not be combined without stating the assumptions.

IntServ、RSVP 与 DiffServ

IntServ细粒度逐流状态、RSVP信令；DiffServ聚类逐跳、可扩展；EF/AF/BE。

IntServ provides support for individual flows along an end-to-end path. Resource reservation can give a flow specific treatment, but routers must maintain relevant state. RSVP is a signalling protocol used to request and maintain reservations; it does not itself carry the application data.

DiffServ groups traffic into classes and applies per-hop treatment to those classes. Packet markings help devices identify the intended class. The course includes best effort, assured forwarding and expedited forwarding. Class-based handling is generally more scalable than maintaining a reservation for every flow.

The approaches trade detailed individual control against scalability and operational complexity. A marking alone does not guarantee end-to-end performance. Devices and domains along the path need appropriate policies, capacity and support. Select the approach according to flow requirements and the size of the network.

Resource control 与 traffic conditioning

优先级、准入；classification→metering→shaping/dropping；shaping加等待。

Resource control determines how limited network resources are used. Prioritization ranks traffic according to importance or urgency. Admission control may refuse a new flow when accepting it would prevent existing commitments from being met.

Traffic conditioning includes classification, metering, shaping and dropping. Classification identifies the traffic class. Metering measures rates or bursts and compares them with a profile, such as an SLA. Shaping delays packets to make a flow fit an allowed profile. Dropping discards selected packets.

These mechanisms have different effects. Shaping can smooth traffic but adds waiting time and needs buffers. Dropping may protect other traffic but harms the discarded flow. The designer should choose behaviour according to application tolerance and policy. For example, delaying a flexible backup may be acceptable, while the same delay could make an interactive application unusable.

Queue 与 scheduling

queue存，schedule选；FIFO到达先后；priority饿死风险；RR轮流；WFQ权重。

Queuing stores packets while they wait for processing or transmission. Scheduling decides which waiting packet or queue is served next. These are related functions, but they are not identical.

FIFO serves packets in arrival order and is simple. Strict priority serves higher-priority traffic first, which can reduce delay for that class but may leave lower-priority traffic waiting under sustained load. Round robin cycles through queues and serves each in turn. Weighted fair queuing assigns different shares according to configured weights.

Queue management, including RED or WRED, can discard packets before a buffer becomes completely full to help control congestion. The choice should reflect service priorities and fairness. A larger buffer may absorb a burst but can also increase delay. Test behaviour under mixed traffic rather than assuming one queue policy meets every requirement.

SLA、policy 与 QoS 的闭环

SLA约定；policy翻译组织目标；QoS执行；measurement验证反馈。

A service level agreement, or SLA, records expected service and the provider’s responsibilities. Performance elements may include data rate, burst tolerance, upstream and downstream service, delay and RMA. A useful SLA also needs clear conditions and measurement methods.

Policies connect the organization’s management goals with rules about resources, access and service priorities. QoS mechanisms enforce relevant treatment at network devices. Monitoring then checks whether the delivered service matches the commitment and provides feedback for adjustment.

For example, a teaching service may have an agreed delay target and capacity level. A policy gives it suitable priority, devices implement that policy, and measurements verify the result. An SLA document alone does not reserve resources. Equally, enabling QoS without a clear goal does not show that users are receiving the service they need.

Measure peak overlap rather than relying only on daily totals. A shared bottleneck may use class-based treatment and a scheduler with suitable shares. Backups can be shaped or moved to a quieter period when their timing is flexible. Admission control may be needed before accepting stronger commitments. Test whether protected traffic meets its target while lower-priority traffic still receives an acceptable service. If a server or external link is the real bottleneck, local queue changes alone will not solve the problem.

Evaluation and conclusion

Verify performance at the service endpoints as well as important links. Record the load, direction and measurement period. Compare the result with the SLA and adjust capacity, rules or expectations when necessary. This closes the loop from requirements to mechanisms and evidence, while keeping the cost and complexity proportionate to the organization’s needs.

Introduction

先说明本题的目标与主线。

Addressing and routing provide structured connectivity between users, applications and devices. Their design should follow the organization’s locations, traffic relationships, growth and service requirements. Addresses are not simply labels added after buying equipment. A coherent plan can make routing, aggregation, management and security easier, while a poor plan can create unnecessary complexity. The designer should state the original allocations, subnet requirements, administrative boundaries and recovery expectations before selecting detailed routing mechanisms. Existing systems and the ability of staff to operate the result must also be considered.

MAC、IP、IPv4 与 IPv6

MAC链路层；IP逻辑网络层；IPv4 32位、IPv6 128位。

A MAC address is used for data-link communication, such as delivery of Ethernet frames on a local link. An IP address is a logical network-layer address used to identify an interface and support delivery between networks.

IPv4 addresses contain 32 bits, commonly written as four decimal octets. Each octet contains eight bits. IPv6 addresses contain 128 bits and are commonly written in hexadecimal groups. IPv6 therefore provides a much larger address space.

Addressing must be planned together with routing, location and network structure. The address plan affects aggregation, management and security rules. For example, a computer sending to a remote network normally delivers the local frame to a gateway while the IP packet identifies the final remote destination. Knowing the roles of both addresses helps explain communication across multiple links.

Classful、CIDR 与 prefix

旧A/B/C固定边界；CIDR显式/p；hostbits=32-p；mask连续1。

Classful addressing used address classes with predetermined network boundaries. The familiar A, B and C classes used /8, /16 and /24 boundaries. This was inflexible and could waste address space or create blocks too small for users’ needs.

Classless Interdomain Routing, or CIDR, uses an explicit prefix length. In IPv4, /p means that p of the 32 bits form the network prefix and the remaining bits form the host portion. The boundary can fall inside an octet.

The subnet mask has ones for the prefix bits and zeros for host bits. For example, /26 corresponds to 255.255.255.192 and leaves six host bits. The address alone is not enough to determine its subnet: the prefix is essential. Do not infer a modern subnet size only from the first octet’s historical class.

Subnetting 通用步骤

先确认原block/需求→borrow→newmask→数量/主机→边界→验证。

Start with the original network and prefix, then state the required subnet count and usable hosts. For equal-sized subnets, choose enough borrowed bits b so that 2 to the power b meets the subnet requirement. The new prefix is the original prefix plus b.

The remaining host bits h equal 32 minus the new prefix. Each ordinary subnet has 2 to the power h addresses and normally two fewer usable host addresses because network and broadcast addresses are reserved.

Find the subnet boundaries from the new prefix. For each subnet, identify its network address, broadcast address, first usable address and last usable address. Finally, check that the blocks do not overlap, stay within the original allocation and meet the requirements. State any special assumptions instead of applying the ordinary usable-host rule blindly.

Routing boundaries 与 AS

AS共同策略；IGP内部，BGP跨AS；FA/WG、hard/soft。

An autonomous system, or AS, is a group of IP prefixes under a common administrative routing policy. An interior gateway protocol, or IGP, distributes routing information inside an AS. RIP and OSPF are examples. BGP is used for routing policy and reachability between autonomous systems, although it also has an internal form.

Routing boundaries divide a network physically or logically according to requirements. Physical examples include interfaces, isolation LANs and DMZs. Logical examples include functional areas, workgroups and administrative domains.

The course describes boundaries between autonomous systems as hard boundaries and internal divisions as soft boundaries. Functional areas can group similar work, while workgroups may share location or applications. Boundaries help organize hierarchy, management and security. Their placement should follow requirements and flows rather than being chosen only because a convenient device is available.

Static 与 dynamic routing

静态简单低协议开销人工维护；动态适应但开销/收敛/技能。

Static routes are configured explicitly. They avoid the control traffic and processing needed to exchange dynamic routing updates. They can be useful for a small, stable or stub network with a simple exit.

Dynamic routing protocols exchange reachability information and adapt routes when topology changes. This supports larger or more interconnected networks, but it uses bandwidth, processor time and memory. The designer must also consider convergence, stability and staff skills.

A static route does not automatically change simply because a better path appears. Maintaining many static routes can become difficult and error-prone. Conversely, adding a complex dynamic protocol to a simple network can create unnecessary work. Select the simplest approach that meets the actual resilience, growth and policy requirements. Re-evaluate it as hierarchy and interconnectivity increase.

RIP、OSPF 与 BGP 选择

RIP distance vector/hop15；OSPF link-state/areas；BGP path-vector/policy；评价收敛开销稳定规模。

RIP is a distance-vector IGP that uses hop count. Its maximum usable hop count is 15, and it can be slow to converge. Its simplicity suits limited networks but restricts scalability.

OSPF is a link-state IGP. It supports areas and is better suited to larger hierarchical networks, although operation is more complex. BGP is a path-vector protocol that supports policy-based routing between autonomous systems. It is important for inter-domain connectivity.

Protocol evaluation should consider convergence time, bandwidth overhead, processor and memory use, stability, scalability, hierarchy and interconnectivity. The choice should also fit service requirements and management skills. A network requiring fast recovery and many alternative paths places different demands on routing from a small stub network. No protocol is best for every role; internal routing and inter-domain routing solve different problems.

Applied example

For example, assign a university’s sites and functions suitable non-overlapping address blocks, leaving realistic room for growth. Calculate subnet sizes from both host counts and subnet counts, then verify network and broadcast boundaries. A small stable site with one exit may use a simple default or static route. A large internal network with multiple paths may justify OSPF, while an inter-AS policy requirement may justify BGP. Do not deploy every protocol merely because it is available. Test reachability, failure recovery and required access restrictions.

Evaluation and conclusion

The final architecture should connect address organization with routing policy and management visibility. Record why each protocol or boundary is needed. Review decisions as hierarchy, interconnectivity and service needs change. A successful plan meets connectivity and resilience requirements while keeping operational cost and complexity under control.

Introduction

A deployed network needs continuing observation, control and support. Network management should connect service goals to reliable information and appropriate actions. Its design must identify what is managed, where information is collected, how it reaches management systems and how staff respond. A multi-campus network also needs to balance a common service view with local knowledge and resilience. Management is not free: its traffic, processing, storage and access rights can affect the network that it is intended to support.

管理层级与 FCAPS

业务→服务→网络→同类元素集合→单设备；FCAPS五域。

The course views network management as several levels. Business management handles budgets, resources and planning. Service management concerns delivered services, such as access bandwidth and application delivery. Network management covers the network as a whole. Element management handles groups of similar devices, while network-element management concerns an individual device.

FCAPS describes five management areas: fault, configuration, accounting, performance and security management. These include detecting faults, controlling settings, tracking usage, measuring service and managing protection.

The levels and functions connect high-level goals with concrete device parameters. For example, a business commitment to reliable teaching leads to a service target, network monitoring and configuration, and specific interface or device actions. Management must also transport and store the information needed to make those decisions, so its own traffic and infrastructure need planning.

SNMP、agent 与 MIB

manager读/设；agent暴露对象；MIB定义；poll/trap；counter差值。

SNMP supports the collection and configuration of management information from network devices. A management system communicates with an agent on a managed device. The agent provides access to managed objects and may send event notifications.

A Management Information Base, or MIB, defines the managed objects and their meaning. It is not the same as a historical database of every collected measurement. For example, MIB-II includes interface counters such as received and transmitted octets and error counts.

Managers can poll for values, while traps provide unsolicited event notification. Counter values often need processing: a rate normally comes from the change in a counter over a known interval, not from the total counter alone. SNMPv3 adds security capabilities, but settings and access restrictions still need to be planned. Choose objects that support actual service and operational needs.

Monitoring、instrumentation、configuration

monitor取/处理/展示/归档；instrument工具接入；configuration修改。

Monitoring obtains and processes information about network behaviour. It includes collection, event or trend analysis, display and archiving. Measurements may concern an element, link, network or end-to-end path.

Instrumentation provides the tools and access needed to obtain that information. Examples include SNMP access, counters, probes, packet capture, ping and traceroute. Accuracy and dependability matter, so measurements from different locations may need comparison.

Configuration changes operational parameters through direct access, remote access or configuration files. It can implement a response to information found by monitoring. For example, instrumentation supplies interface data, monitoring detects sustained congestion, and configuration changes an appropriate queue policy. These mechanisms form a practical management loop. Observing a problem does not itself fix it, and a successful connectivity probe does not establish every aspect of service quality.

Event notification 与 trend analysis

短期阈值快速响应；长期baseline容量规划；短poll更多开销，平均可能藏事件。

Event notification identifies changes that need attention, such as failures, threshold crossings or important informational events. Short-lived problems may require frequent collection and prompt alarms, logs or displays.

Trend analysis studies longer-term behaviour. Continuous and consistent measurements establish a baseline and reveal changes in utilization, delay or availability. This supports planning before service becomes unacceptable.

The methods use different time scales. A daily average may hide a brief outage, while a stream of isolated alarms may not show gradual growth. Shorter polling intervals improve time resolution but create more traffic, processing and stored data. Longer intervals reduce overhead but may miss short events. A useful design combines event and trend information and records timestamps, units and context so that measurements can be interpreted correctly.

Management traffic scaling

D×I×P×(value+overhead)×8/T；存储未必含wire overhead；峰值另算。

Estimate the number of devices, interfaces per device, parameters per interface and polling frequency. Multiply the number of measurements by the bytes generated per measurement, including relevant protocol overhead, to estimate traffic per polling cycle. Divide by the interval to obtain an average rate.

Storage is a separate calculation. It depends on which values and metadata are kept, their size and the retention period. Network protocol overhead is not automatically stored with every value. Polling bursts may also create higher short-term load than the average suggests.

The lecture gives LAN planning guidelines of roughly 2–5% management load and suggests review when it exceeds 10%. These are course heuristics, not universal limits. Reduce unnecessary parameters or polling frequency, distribute collection and use suitable aggregation. Critical management information still needs sufficient resources and timely delivery.

Centralized、distributed、hierarchical management

集中简易但聚合/故障风险；分布本地处理复杂协调；层级分工汇总。

Centralized management collects control and information at a main management system. It can simplify administration and provide a common view, but it can concentrate traffic and create a significant failure point unless protected.

Distributed management places systems across different domains or locations. Local processing can improve scalability and reduce dependence on a single site, but coordination and consistent policy become harder. Hierarchical management separates responsibilities across levels, allowing local collection and higher-level summaries or control.

Choose the architecture according to network size, geography, reliability, staffing and cost. For example, a multi-campus network may benefit from local collection with a central service view. Distribution does not automatically remove every failure risk, and centralization does not forbid redundancy. State where information is collected, stored and acted on, and how those management systems remain reachable.

Applied example

For a university, local collectors can monitor campus links and important devices while higher-level systems combine service information. Use frequent event detection where brief failures matter and longer-term records for growth planning. Protect management credentials and restrict configuration rights. In-band management uses the production network, so it may become unreachable when that path fails. An independent out-of-band path can provide recovery access, but only if its own links, power and systems remain available. It also requires security and maintenance. Compare measurements from different points and keep timestamps and object definitions so that a counter error does not trigger a wrong operational decision.

Evaluation and conclusion

Evaluate the management service itself. Check alarm usefulness, data accuracy, polling overhead, recovery access and the ability of staff to carry out procedures. Preserve useful local operation if a higher-level system fails. The preferred design is the one that provides dependable control and evidence at a manageable cost, with clear responsibilities and tested failure behaviour.

Introduction

先说明本题的目标与主线。

A network architecture needs several views because geography, traffic relationships and service functions describe different aspects of the same system. A reference architecture combines these views with the component architectures selected from requirements and flow analysis. Its purpose is to make the complete network coherent. The designer should explain not only what functions are present but also how their mechanisms help or constrain one another. The balance depends on the organization’s priorities, rather than on maximizing every individual feature in isolation.

课堂讨论的全部架构模型

课堂讨论三组架构模型。拓扑类包含LAN/MAN/WAN，即按地理尺度组织网络，以及access/distribution/core，即按功能层次组织网络。 流量类描述通信模式。对等模式使通信实体处于相近层级；客户端—服务器模式关注双方通信；分层客户端—服务器加入服务器层级及层间通信；分布式计算描述协作计算资源及其数据交换。 功能类围绕服务或访问要求组织架构。服务提供方模型关注服务交付、隐私和安全等提供方功能；内网/外网模型根据内部使用和被许可的外部使用划分访问；端到端模型考虑完整流量路径上的所有组件。 这些视角可以组合。设计者可从拓扑模型开始，添加流量与功能视角，形成满足需求的参考架构。这些是互补视角，不是单一通用解法。

The lectures discuss three groups of architectural models. Topological models include LAN/MAN/WAN, which organizes the network by geographical scale, and access/distribution/core, which organizes it by functional layers.

Flow-based models describe communication patterns. Peer-to-peer places communicating entities at a similar level. Client-server focuses on communication between clients and servers. Hierarchical client-server adds server tiers and communication between them. Distributed computing describes cooperating computing resources and their data exchanges.

Functional models organize the architecture around services or access requirements. The service-provider model focuses on provider functions such as service delivery, privacy and security. The intranet/extranet model separates access according to internal and permitted external use. The end-to-end model considers all components along a complete traffic path.

These views can be combined. A designer can start with a topological model and add flow-based and functional views to form a reference architecture that meets the requirements. They are complementary views rather than a single universal solution.

Reference architecture 的形成

完整功能/内部外部关系；topological打底，再叠加flow/functional。

A reference architecture describes the complete network architecture. It combines the selected component architectures, their mechanisms and the internal and external relationships that affect the whole system.

The course suggests starting with a topological model, such as LAN/MAN/WAN or access/distribution/core. Flow-based models then describe important communication relationships. Functional models add perspectives such as service delivery, intranet/extranet separation or complete end-to-end paths.

These models are complementary views. For example, a campus can use an access/distribution/core structure, support client-server flows and provide controlled extranet access for partners. The designer then checks how routing, management, performance and security work together within that structure. Requirements and flow priorities determine the balance. A reference architecture is therefore more than a topology drawing or a collection of separate product choices.

内部关系与外部关系

同一功能内部 vs 不同功能之间；trade-off/dependency/constraint。

Internal relationships exist between mechanisms within one component architecture. They include trade-offs, dependencies and constraints. For example, within performance architecture, a policy may depend on QoS mechanisms to enforce a service level agreement.

External relationships exist between different component architectures. For example, security inspection can affect performance, management traffic depends on addressing and routing, and management access can create security exposure.

The classification depends on the functions being compared, not simply whether the devices are in the same building. A reference architecture combines the component architectures and their relationships. It must balance them according to requirements and flow priorities. Optimizing each component alone may give a poor overall result. For instance, extensive inspection may improve one security goal while making a critical application miss its delay requirement.

安全架构的 trade-offs

内部机制依赖/约束；security↔performance、management、addressing。

Within security architecture, mechanisms can depend on or constrain one another. Encryption requires key management, while address-based rules depend on accurate addressing information. Combining mechanisms therefore needs more than selecting each one separately.

Security also interacts with other component architectures. Inspection and encryption can add processing work and delay. Security rules can block management traffic, even though monitoring and configuration are needed to verify protection. Dynamic addressing and NAT can make address-based access rules and logs harder to interpret.

A designer should compare these effects with the priorities identified during analysis. Selective protection and well-placed zones can protect important assets while controlling unnecessary overhead. Measure the resulting service rather than assuming every security mechanism has the same cost. Document the remaining risks and review the balance when applications or threats change.

SLA、policy 与 QoS 的闭环

SLA约定；policy翻译组织目标；QoS执行；measurement验证反馈。

A service level agreement, or SLA, records expected service and the provider’s responsibilities. Performance elements may include data rate, burst tolerance, upstream and downstream service, delay and RMA. A useful SLA also needs clear conditions and measurement methods.

Policies connect the organization’s management goals with rules about resources, access and service priorities. QoS mechanisms enforce relevant treatment at network devices. Monitoring then checks whether the delivered service matches the commitment and provides feedback for adjustment.

For example, a teaching service may have an agreed delay target and capacity level. A policy gives it suitable priority, devices implement that policy, and measurements verify the result. An SLA document alone does not reserve resources. Equally, enabling QoS without a clear goal does not show that users are receiving the service they need.

布局、traceability 与成功指标

逻辑关系vs物理蓝图；component plan功能视角；需求→架构→选择→metric。

Network layout combines topology, technology, equipment, relationships and strategic locations. Logical diagrams emphasize connectivity and service relationships. Physical blueprints describe actual devices, cables, rooms and security locations. Component plans isolate a function, such as security or network management, for clearer explanation.

Design traceability links problem statements and requirements to architecture and detailed design decisions. This creates evidence that choices are objective and defensible. It also helps assess the effect of a changed requirement.

Success metrics check whether the design achieves its goals. Capacity, delay and RMA are important, but user and device requirements may add other measures. For example, a teaching-service requirement should connect to a traffic policy, suitable devices and a defined performance test. A diagram or equipment list alone does not prove that the final service meets the requirement.

Applied example

AI补充校园案例：把前面机制连成方案，并保留条件。

A campus may use an access/distribution/core topology, client-server teaching services, distributed research computing and controlled partner access. These are compatible views, not competing names for one physical layout. Place important mechanisms where the flows require them. For instance, public services may need a separate security zone, interactive teaching may need differentiated treatment, and management needs controlled access across those boundaries. Check whether encryption or inspection adds unacceptable delay, whether address changes confuse access rules and whether management collection competes with important traffic. The chosen compromise should preserve primary requirements. If a mechanism cannot deliver the agreed service, revise the component plan or the service commitment and record the reason.

Evaluation and conclusion

用可验证结果检验方案，保留取舍和适用条件。

Use the reference architecture to guide detailed choices and tests. Each important relationship should be traceable to a requirement or constraint. This allows reviewers to understand why the combination was chosen and helps future changes preserve the network’s overall purpose.

Introduction

先说明本题的目标与主线。

Programmable and software-based networking can make service changes easier to coordinate. However, technology should be selected because it meets requirements, not simply because it is new. SDN changes how forwarding is controlled. SD-WAN applies software-based management to wide-area connectivity. NFV changes how network functions are delivered. Automation performs life-cycle tasks, while machine learning uses data or experience to support predictions and decisions. These ideas can work together, but they are not interchangeable and none removes the need for sound architecture and verification.

SDN 三平面

application意图/服务；controller全局策略；data匹配转发/状态回报。

SDN separates the application, control and data planes. The application plane contains network services and applications that express requirements and use a view of the network.

The control plane is managed by one or more controllers. It maintains information about the infrastructure, makes or implements policy decisions and programs forwarding behaviour. The data plane contains physical or virtual forwarding devices. They process traffic according to rules and report relevant network state.

The separation allows control logic and forwarding to evolve more independently. For example, an application can request a traffic policy, the controller translates it into device rules, and switches apply those rules to packets. This supports programmability and coordinated management. A logically centralized view does not require one unprotected physical controller. Controller architecture, communication and failure handling remain important design decisions.

SD-WAN

软件管理广域网；应用体验/云连接/安全/简化；仍依赖底层链路。

SD-WAN applies a software-based management approach to a wide area network. A WAN connects geographically separated networks, such as the LANs of different campuses or offices.

SD-WAN can make policy management more consistent and improve how applications use available WAN connectivity. The course highlights better application experience, stronger managed security, optimized cloud connectivity and simpler management. These are potential benefits rather than outcomes guaranteed by the name alone.

The design must still consider the capacity, delay, loss and availability of the underlying links. It also needs suitable security policies, monitoring and controller resilience. For example, centrally applying a policy for teaching traffic can simplify operation across campuses, but it cannot remove a physical outage unless an alternative path exists. Select SD-WAN when its capabilities address the organization’s flows and management needs at an acceptable cost.

SDN 与 Big Data 的双向关系

5V；网络可编程/全局调度支持数据；数据分析反馈TE/安全；规模瓶颈。

Big data is commonly described by volume, variety, velocity, value and veracity. These concern how much data exists, its forms and speed, the value obtained and its trustworthiness.

SDN can support big-data applications through a global network view, programmable control and dynamic reconfiguration. These help coordinate data delivery, resource use and processing across data centres. Network decisions can be adjusted as application demand changes.

The relationship also works in the other direction. Analysis of network data can help SDN with traffic engineering, cross-layer decisions, security detection and data-centre operation. However, useful data does not automatically produce good decisions. The course identifies controller scalability, flow-table management and flexible programming abstractions as open issues. The designer must consider data quality, control overhead and the practical ability to apply decisions in time.

Network automation

配置管理测试部署运维；减少重复/拼写错；验证/分批/回退是AI实施补充。

Network automation uses software to perform tasks in the network life cycle, including configuration, management, testing, deployment and operation. The course gives SDN, Ansible, Puppet and Python scripts as examples of approaches or tools.

Automation can reduce repetitive manual work, typing errors and inconsistent configuration. It can improve speed, scalability and network-wide policy consistency. It also frees engineers to focus on tasks that need judgement.

However, an incorrect automated instruction can repeat the same mistake across many devices. Access control, accurate input and verification are still needed. As a practical extension, testing changes, deploying them in stages and preparing rollback help manage this risk. Automation is broader than SDN: it can be used on traditional devices as well. Its success should be measured by reliable outcomes, not only by how quickly commands are sent.

NFV 与三要素

软件网络功能脱离专有盒子；infrastructure资源；VNF功能；MANO管理编排。

Network Function Virtualization, or NFV, implements network functions as software on shared, general-purpose infrastructure. For example, a firewall or load-balancing function can run as a software instance instead of requiring a separate dedicated appliance for every deployment.

The course identifies three main elements. NFV infrastructure, or NFVI, provides the computing, storage, networking and virtualization resources. Virtual network functions, or VNFs, provide the actual network functions. Management and orchestration, or MANO, manages their deployment and life cycle and coordinates resources.

Separating functions from dedicated equipment can improve flexibility and allow services to be placed or scaled when needed. However, performance, isolation, shared-resource contention and infrastructure failure still matter. Virtualization changes how functions are delivered; it does not remove the requirement to test service capacity, availability and security.

ML workflow、应用与挑战

历史数据→建模/评价/优化循环→新数据预测；routing/IDS/QoS/resources；数据偏差隐私算力。

An ML workflow starts with suitable historical data. Model building, evaluation and optimization form an iterative process. After evaluation, the model can make predictions or support decisions on new data. As a practical extension, new conditions should be monitored because old patterns may no longer represent the network.

Networking applications include predicting traffic volume or link quality, intrusion detection, routing support, QoS improvement and resource allocation. These applications can assist engineers but still require suitable operational controls.

The course highlights computational load and the trade-off with accuracy, data availability and privacy, imbalanced datasets and the need for realistic testing. A model trained mostly on normal traffic may perform poorly on rare attacks. Evaluate results against the actual task and consider the consequences of wrong predictions. More complex models are not automatically better for every network.

Suppose a university needs to extend services across campuses and respond to changing research demand. SD-WAN may help coordinate WAN policies. NFV can provide software firewall functions, while SDN can steer relevant traffic through them. This shows the difference between virtualizing a function and controlling connectivity. Automation can apply a reviewed configuration consistently. Network data can support traffic prediction, but predictions need evaluation before they affect critical services. Preserve secure control access and consider controller or shared-infrastructure failures. A fast automated change can spread a mistake, and a confident ML result can still be wrong when data is incomplete or unlike the training data.

Assess the combined system using service outcomes, operational effort, security, resilience and cost. Begin with a clear task and verify that the organization can support the new dependencies. Programmability creates options; disciplined requirements, testing and management turn those options into a useful network service.

Network design turns analysis and architecture into a solution that can be built, operated and assessed. A successful design is more than a shopping list and more than a high score in a comparison table. It must satisfy primary requirements, work within constraints and provide evidence linking decisions to organizational needs. Evaluation, layout, documentation and testing therefore belong to one connected process. The designer should be able to explain why a particular solution was chosen and how users will know that it works.

供应商、设备和服务评价

Begin with the requirements and reference architecture. Define the evaluation goals, criteria and rules before comparing candidates. Gather and organize documentation, discuss relevant questions and collect evidence about each option.

Develop a consistent rating method. Criteria may include cost, technology, performance, risk and scalability. Weights should reflect agreed priorities, and scores should be supported by comparable evidence. Ratings and ranking should be made independently of the vendors being assessed.

The preferred option must meet essential requirements and provide a suitable overall balance. Purchase price alone is not enough: support, reputation, backup resources, operating costs and compatibility also matter. Keep the process transparent and document why candidates are accepted or rejected. If the evidence shows that no candidate meets a primary requirement, review the options or requirements rather than hiding that failure inside a favourable average score.

Primary design criteria are conditions required for project success. Secondary criteria are desirable features that may be sacrificed when they conflict with essential goals. This distinction gives the design a clear priority structure.

Technology selection is constrained by cost, existing networks and facilities. Flow specifications provide service and capacity requirements. A large network can be divided into manageable sections, with each section evaluated against its own needs and interfaces. Concept elements include business environment, technology choices, scalability, dependability, security, management and maintainability. Compatibility with legacy systems, limits, flexibility, geography, finances, risk, performance and simplicity also matter. These define the conditions the solution must satisfy.

Technology elements describe nodes, links, topology, interfaces, services, protocols and traffic mapping. They explain what kinds of components connect, how services overlay the network and where traffic requirements apply. For example, a multi-campus design needs geographical reach, growth and availability goals as well as suitable link types and protocols. Architectural design should be specific enough to guide choices without being confused with a final list of device models, cable lengths and installation details.

Traceability links design decisions to requirements and architectural decisions. It helps reviewers understand why a choice was made and whether it has a clear basis.

Operational suitability asks whether the customer can operate the planned network. It depends on both the design and the skills of the people who will use and run it.

Supportability asks what is needed to maintain effective operation after delivery. The course identifies RMA, workforce and training, procedures and documentation, tools, and spare or repair parts. A system that performs well on installation day may still be hard to support later.

Confidence, in this course, concerns the network’s ability to deliver data at the required rate without unacceptable error or loss. The acceptable level depends on the application. These requirements extend the design beyond nominal link speed. For example, a complex device may offer high performance but require training, maintenance procedures and spare parts that the organization cannot currently provide.
