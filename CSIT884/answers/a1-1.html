How do I register for a study workshop?

2026001, mayal@uowmail.edu.au

  • Choose a suitable workshop from the list and note when it will run.
  • Fill in the form with your student number and university email address.
  • Check that you chose the right workshop and entered the correct contact details.
  • Send the request before registration closes.
  • Look in your university email for the confirmation.
